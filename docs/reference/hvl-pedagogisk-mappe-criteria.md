## 0. How to read this file

- Quotes are verbatim and keep their original language. Most HVL merit pages are in Bokmål. The Nynorsk
  wording comes from HVL's news article on the 2025 awardees.
- **Confidence:** *high* means two or more separate HVL pages or documents agree. *medium* means one
  official page, quoted through a search excerpt. *low* means a paraphrase or an inference.
  Nothing here is *high* in the strict sense of "seen directly", because no page could be opened.
- No PDF named *retningslinjer for pedagogisk mappe* turned up in any search. HVL publishes this
  guidance as **web pages**: the merit call, the applicant FAQ, ALU's portfolio page and the
  qualification rules. ALU may still hold an internal PDF *(verify)*.

## 1. Summary

1. **The criteria are not numbered 1.1–3.2.** The HVL 2025 merit call (*Merittert underviser – utlysning 2025*)
   has three main criteria and **eleven** sub-criteria: **1.1–1.4, 2.1–2.4 and 3.1–3.3**. HVL notes that they
   were *revised* for 2025 and differ from earlier calls. The 2023 call had a different list. The plan's
   "1.1–3.2" probably comes from an earlier or partial list. *(medium)*
2. **The merit portfolio has three parts:** Del 1, a continuous text of **up to 7 500 words**
   (references excluded); Del 2, a *Pedagogisk CV* (your own CV or HVL's template); and Del 3, **up to 11
   attachments**, one of which must be the manager's statement. *(high: the 2023 call, the 2025 call and the FAQ agree)*
3. **Educational technology** is explicitly in scope. ALU says the portfolio covers work with
   educational technology *(medium, paraphrase)*. Merit sub-criterion **2.3** names *"varierte læremidler"*
   (varied learning materials) *(medium)*. The list of suggested attachments includes *"dokumentasjon og
   eksempler på utvikling av læringsressurser"* *(medium)*. No separate HVL rule on documenting
   digital competence was found.
4. **National basis:** the merit scheme comes from Meld. St. 16 (2016–2017) *Kultur for kvalitet i
   høyere utdanning*. The portfolio for appointment and promotion comes from the regulation:
   universitets- og høyskoleforskriften, § 3-8 on *utdanningsfaglig kompetanse*. UHR has advisory
   guidelines for *utdanningsfaglig kompetanse* and recommends documenting it in a *pedagogisk mappe*.
   **No source says that HVL's merit criteria derive from a UHR document**, and no single national UHR
   set of merit criteria was found.
